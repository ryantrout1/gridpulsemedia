## What this service is

Grid Pulse Media runs ongoing marketing for independent auto repair shops. Your website, Google profile, social posts and listings show the same hours, services and phone number, so a driver with a warning light finds you and trusts you.

## Who this suits

Shop owners who are under a car all day. Shops that earn great reviews in person but rarely ask for them online. Anyone whose Google hours or services do not match the website.

## Built on trust

People pick a mechanic they trust. We answer every review in your voice, explain the work you do in plain words on your website, and post the kind of helpful tips that show customers you are honest. We never claim a price, a warranty or a turnaround time you have not given us.

## What to expect after

Once you start, we write your 90-day plan: what to say, where it goes and when. You read it, ask for changes, and nothing goes out until you approve it. Then posts publish on schedule, reviews and comments get answered in your voice, and your hours and details stay matching on your website, Google profile and the directories customers check. Every 90 days we look at what worked and plan the next stretch.

## How to start

Fill out the short Get started form, about five minutes, with your business basics and any website, Google profile or social accounts you already have. We read it before we talk, so the first call is about your business, not paperwork.

[Get started](https://www.gridpulsemedia.com/start/) and we will take it from there.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you help us get more Google reviews?

We help you build a simple habit of asking, and we answer every review that comes in, in your voice.

### Do you list our services and specialties?

Yes, from what you tell us: the makes you work on, the services you offer and anything you specialize in.

### Do I need a website and social accounts already?

No. Start with whatever you have, even nothing. The Get started form asks for your website, Google profile and social accounts, and every one of them is optional. Anything missing gets set up once you start.

### How much does this cost?

It is $1,500 a month with no setup fee. You commit to three months to start, then it runs month to month, and the website is yours to keep. The Get started form takes about five minutes.
