## What this service is

Grid Pulse Media runs ongoing marketing for commercial real estate firms: investment sponsors, brokers and property owners. Your website, Google profile, articles and social posts all tell the same story about how you buy, what you look for and how you treat the people you work with. The goal is simple: when an investor, a seller or a broker looks you up, they find a firm that is active, clear and easy to trust.

## Who this suits

Firms where trust decides everything and the owner is too busy closing deals to write. Sponsors who raise money from investors and want a steady stream of market notes and lessons learned instead of a website that has not changed in two years. Brokers and owners who want sellers and partners to find them on Google with the right details.

## What we write about

Market updates on the cities you invest in, lessons from deals you passed on or closed, how you think about risk, what you look for in a property, and answers to the questions investors ask before their first call. Everything is written from what you tell us, in your own voice.

## What we never do

We never state a return, a rent figure or a timeline as a promise. Numbers are written as projections or targets, the way you and your counsel want them. We never invent results, property performance or investor outcomes, and we never name a client, a partner or a property you have not approved for public use. Words like guaranteed or risk-free are blocked before anything is written. Your offering rules come first, and you approve every piece before it goes out.

## What to expect after

Once you start, we write your 90-day plan: what to say, where it goes and when. You read it, ask for changes, and nothing goes out until you approve it. Then posts publish on schedule, reviews and comments get answered in your voice, and your hours and details stay matching on your website, Google profile and the directories customers check. Every 90 days we look at what worked and plan the next stretch. LinkedIn, where much of this audience spends its time, is part of the plan too.

## How to start

Fill out the short Get started form, about five minutes, with your business basics and any website, Google profile or social accounts you already have. We read it before we talk, so the first call is about your business, not paperwork.

[Get started](https://www.gridpulsemedia.com/start/) and we will take it from there.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you write about our deals without sharing anything confidential?

Yes. We write only from what you tell us is fine to share, and we never name a client, partner or property without your approval. A story about a deal you passed on can teach the lesson without naming the deal.

### Will you make claims about returns?

No. Returns, rents and timelines are written as projections or targets, never promises, and phrases like guaranteed or risk-free are blocked. You approve every piece before it is published.

### Do I need a website and social accounts already?

No. Start with whatever you have, even nothing. The Get started form asks for your website, Google profile and social accounts, and every one of them is optional. Anything missing gets set up once you start.

### How much does this cost?

It is $1,500 a month with no setup fee. You commit to three months to start, then it runs month to month, and the website is yours to keep. The Get started form takes about five minutes.
