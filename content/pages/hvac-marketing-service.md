## What this service is

Grid Pulse Media runs ongoing marketing for heating and air conditioning companies. Your website, Google profile, social posts and directory listings all say the same thing, so a homeowner with a broken unit finds the right phone number and the right hours the first time.

## Who this suits

Owners whose crews are booked solid in peak season and who have no time to post. Companies that go quiet online in the slow months and then scramble when the first heat wave or cold snap hits. Anyone whose Google hours or service area do not match the website.

## Planned around your seasons

HVAC work follows the weather, so the plan does too. Before the busy season we talk about tune-ups and maintenance plans. During it we keep your details right and answer reviews. In the slow months we write the articles that help you get found when the next rush starts.

## What to expect after

Once you start, we write your 90-day plan: what to say, where it goes and when. You read it, ask for changes, and nothing goes out until you approve it. Then posts publish on schedule, reviews and comments get answered in your voice, and your hours and details stay matching on your website, Google profile and the directories customers check. Every 90 days we look at what worked and plan the next stretch.

## How to start

Fill out the short Get started form, about five minutes, with your business basics and any website, Google profile or social accounts you already have. We read it before we talk, so the first call is about your business, not paperwork.

[Get started](https://www.gridpulsemedia.com/start/) and we will take it from there.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you promote our maintenance plans and seasonal tune-ups?

Yes, using the details you give us: what is included, the price if you want it shown, and when to push it. We never post an offer or price you have not told us.

### What if our hours change in peak season?

Tell us once and the change goes to your website, Google profile and social accounts so they all match.

### Do I need a website and social accounts already?

No. Start with whatever you have, even nothing. The Get started form asks for your website, Google profile and social accounts, and every one of them is optional. Anything missing gets set up once you start.

### How much does this cost?

It is $1,500 a month with no setup fee. You commit to three months to start, then it runs month to month, and the website is yours to keep. The Get started form takes about five minutes.
